Display Response Curve Tool

How to Use

  1. Adjust the Reference Brightness (p) slider. This sets the statistical brightness of the random pattern on the left (e.g., 50% means 50% of pixels are white, 50% are black).
  2. Adjust the Matcher Gray (G) slider until the solid gray patch on the right visually matches the perceived brightness of the left pattern.
  3. Tip: Squinting, blurring your eyes, or viewing from a distance makes matching easier.
  4. When they match, click Record Data Point. This saves the (G, p) pair.
  5. Repeat this for several 'p' values (e.g., 10%, 20%, ... 90%) to build a complete curve.
  6. The plot below shows your measured response (blue dots) against a standard Gamma 2.2 curve (red line).
